FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is BrandPulse?+
An autonomous agent that researches your market, remembers your brand, and produces an evidence-backed strategy from a single objective — without step-by-step approval.
Do I need to hardcode my competitors?+
No. You enter your own competitors, industry and audience on the mission form — nothing is preset or industry-specific.
How does the agent back up its claims?+
Every signal in the Research panel shows its source, the agent's interpretation, and a confidence score. Whitespace opportunities are labeled as hypotheses, never guaranteed facts.
What happens after the strategy is generated?+
An internal critic scores it, flags weak spots, and the agent automatically revises — visible in the Critic & Revision panel and the full Execution Trace.
Does this run without API keys?+
Yes — this build runs in demo mode with clearly labeled simulated research, so the full loop is demonstrable with no configuration.
